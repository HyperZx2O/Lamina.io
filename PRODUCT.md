# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Bangladeshi students (Class 6–10) studying the NCTB national curriculum, using Lamina for self-paced tutoring at home. Teachers using the copilot to generate lesson content, questions, and answers. Bilingual Bengali/English — many users are more comfortable in Bengali but study subjects in English. Working on laptops in home environments, often in the evening. Motivated by exams and curriculum milestones.

## Product Purpose

An AI education assistant that delivers personalized lessons, generates answers and practice questions, and assists teachers — all grounded in the Bangladeshi NCTB national curriculum via RAG. Success is a student understanding a concept they were stuck on, without needing a paid tutor.

## Brand Personality

Modern, Scholarly, Bold. Confident but not arrogant. Academic but not stuffy. Speaks to the student as a capable learner, not a child.

## Anti-references

No corporate/cold EdTech — avoid sterile platforms like Blackboard, Moodle, or generic LMS dashboards. Also not a gamified kids-app. No garish gradients or cartoon mascots.

## Design Principles

1. **Warmth over sterility** — the interface feels like a well-lit study room, not a hospital lobby. Warm dark tones, soft glow, approachable typography.
2. **Bilingual by default** — every piece of UI exists in both English and Bengali. Language is not an afterthought or a toggle-hidden feature.
3. **Curriculum-grounded** — RAG from NCTB textbooks ensures answers are relevant to what students are actually studying, not generic Wikipedia content.
4. **Progressive depth** — the surface is simple (one topic input), but layers of depth unfold as needed (follow-up mode, level adaptation, source retrieval).
5. **Tool, not toy** — respects the student's goal of actually learning. Gamification (streaks, history) supports motivation, not distraction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline. Bilingual interface with proper Bengali typography (Hind Siliguri). Focus-visible indicators, aria labels on interactive elements, semantic roles. Future: prefers-reduced-motion support, light mode.
